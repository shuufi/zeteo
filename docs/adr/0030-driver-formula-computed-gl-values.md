# Operational Driver retires from GLNode; Driver Formula computes GL leaf (and Driver) values via sum-of-products

Financial Performance's decorative Operational Driver rows (ADR-0023, ADR-0029) never actually explained a leaf's dollar amount — the leaf's `gl_fact` and the driver's `gl_fact` were independently randomised, so "driver explains leaf" was narrative parenting only. To let operational drivers (crew complement, rank mix, payroll rate, etc.) genuinely *drive* a leaf's value, we introduce **Driver** (a reusable named quantity, own table, no fixed tree position, valued per company × month × scenario — same grain as `gl_fact`) and **Driver Formula** (a named sum-of-products expression — ordered terms of Drivers chained by `×`/`÷`, terms summed with an optional per-formula sign) that binds to exactly one **target**: either a GL Posting Account leaf or another Driver.

A target's amount is the sum of every Formula bound to it, and this *replaces* any independently-fabricated `gl_fact` row for that leaf — the formula becomes the sole source of truth, not a parallel annotation that could disagree with the P&L. Because a Driver can itself be a target, drivers can recurse (a Driver computed from a Formula of further Drivers), so the tree walk needs cycle detection, the same class of check a circular `parent_code` would need. No unit conversion/validation is performed — a formula author is trusted to combine drivers whose product/sum already lands in the target's unit. Formulas support no raw numeric literals and no parenthesized/nested sub-expressions; a "constant" is modelled as a flat-valued Driver instead, keeping the model to one concept.

`NodeType.OPERATIONAL_DRIVER` retires from `GLNode`/`general_ledger` entirely — the existing 12 `OPD-000N` rows (charter rate, utilization, off-hire days, etc.) migrate into the new Driver table for a single unified concept rather than two competing "driver" mechanisms. Since none of them has a Formula (they're context-only, driving nothing), each keeps a `displayed_under` pointer to the GL leaf it historically explained — a display-only anchor, not part of the compute graph — so they keep rendering in their old spot on Financial Trends.

Visually, expanding a GL leaf shows its bound Formula(s) as child rows; expanding a Formula shows its Driver terms, recursing into further Formulas where a term is itself Formula-driven — the same expand-in-place mechanism the leaf-drilldown already uses (ADR-0029), just extended one (or more) levels deeper.

Rejected: keeping drivers decorative-only and adding formulas as a separate, non-authoritative annotation — this would leave "what the driver math says" and "what the P&L shows" permanently able to disagree. Rejected: a fully general nested/parenthesized expression tree — sum-of-products covers every realistic formula shape here at a fraction of the storage/evaluation/UI complexity. Rejected: allowing raw numeric literals in a formula — keeping every value a Driver, even a flat one, means the same edit/reuse/display machinery covers constants for free instead of needing a second kind of term.

**Status**: accepted
