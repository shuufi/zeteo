# VDT becomes a real, activity-based second hierarchy with its own estimated leaves; the old GL/FSI tree is renamed Accounting hierarchy

Until now "VDT" was just another name for the accounting GL/FSI tree (ADR-0022) — `VdtNode`/`GET /api/gl/tree` and VDT Explorer all rendered the same reporting-structured hierarchy Trends does. Management now wants a genuinely distinct **VDT hierarchy**, organized by activity (e.g. Crew Pay and Benefits Cost, Crew Traveling and Welfare Cost) rather than by GL nature (Manpower Cost, Materials And Supplies), for the pilot scope (Cost of Revenue and Revenue only — see Open Items).

Unlike a straightforward re-parenting, the VDT hierarchy's leaves are **not** the same rows as the Accounting hierarchy's `Posting GL Account`s. Two new node types cover it:

- **Activity Node** — the mid-tier grouping (e.g. "Crew Pay and Benefits Cost" → "Salaries & Allowances" → "Base salary by rank and nationality"), replacing Reporting Node within Cost of Revenue/Revenue. Variable depth. Coded `V` + 9 digits: 1-digit branch (1=Revenue, 2=Cost of Revenue) + up to four 2-digit nesting-position segments (unused levels zero-padded), e.g. `V201000000`. Purely for human legibility — `Parent Code` is the real source of structure.
- **Posting Activity Account** — the VDT hierarchy's terminal line, a named item (e.g. "Senior officer nationality mix") with its own Driver Formula computing its own RM amount, structurally the same shape as a Driver Formula target (ADR-0030) rather than a GL fact row. It carries an `FA GL` pointer to the real GL account it's conceptually explaining — a **display/reconciliation anchor, not identity**. Coded separately and sequentially, `VA` + 8 digits, zero-padded, e.g. `VA00000001` (no positional encoding — its parent Activity Node's code already gives tree position; nobody browses these by position).

**The FA GL anchor is many-to-one and non-reconciling.** Multiple Posting Activity Accounts can anchor to the same real GL account (e.g. "Senior officer nationality mix" / "Junior officer nationality mix" / "Ratings / crew nationality mix" all anchor to `5100100100`, COS-Crew costs-Salary & Wages) — sometimes it's 1:1, sometimes a real GL account explains into several activity lines. First pilot data seeded: `docs/vdt-hierarchy-crew-cost.csv`, 10 Activity Nodes and 21 Posting Activity Accounts under Cost of Revenue's Crew Cost, e.g. `V201000000` "SOC Crew Cost" → ... → `V201010101` "Base salary by rank and nationality" → `VA00000001`/`VA00000002`/`VA00000003` (Senior/Junior/Ratings nationality mix, all anchored to `5100100100`). Their formula-computed amounts are **independent estimates and are not required to sum to the anchor GL account's real `GLFact` total** — this is a deliberate design choice, not a gap to close: VDT's whole value is giving its own bottom-up, activity-driven estimate of the same story Accounting tells top-down from postings, and where the two disagree is itself the diagnostic signal. Consequently:

- The VDT hierarchy's subtotals (rolling up Posting Activity Account estimates through Activity Nodes) are **not guaranteed to equal** the Accounting hierarchy's subtotals for the same scope, even restricted to Cost of Revenue/Revenue where both hierarchies exist. Only outside the pilot scope (G&A Expenses, Other Income & Expenses, Secondary Cost Elements, Taxation) do the two hierarchies share the literal same Reporting Root/Reporting Node rows and therefore reconcile exactly, because nothing there has been re-modelled yet.
- The **reconciliation report** (new route under `/vdt`) is therefore a genuine comparison, not a tautology check: side by side, Accounting's grouping/total vs VDT's grouping/total for the same node/scope/period, showing the gap between them — not a "these must match" validation. This replaces the earlier assumption (same leaf values, pure regrouping) with the more useful "here's where VDT's estimate diverges from the ledger" framing.

Consequences for existing screens, unchanged from the original design:
- **VDT Explorer (`/vdt`) is repointed** to walk the VDT hierarchy. Its root landing view is revamped into a full income-statement layout (same shape as Trends) instead of today's ranked-children-table; existing Ranked (`/vdt/:id`)/Tree (`/vdt-tree/:id`) views are kept for drilling into a specific node below root. Its "amounts" for Cost of Revenue/Revenue are now VDT's own estimates, not Accounting figures.
- **Trends is unaffected** — continues to render the Accounting hierarchy exactly as before, unambiguously its home.
- Driver/Driver Formula leaf decomposition (ADR-0030) on the Accounting side is unaffected. A Posting Activity Account's own Formula is a new, analogous use of the same Formula/Term machinery, but targets a Posting Activity Account instead of a GL Posting Account or Driver — `DriverFormula.target_code`'s polymorphic resolution (GL-code-vs-Driver-code namespace) needs a third case added for Posting Activity Account codes (`VA` + 8 digits, itself non-colliding by prefix).

Terminology retired: **"VDT node"** (`CONTEXT.md`'s old definition, meaning any GL/FSI hierarchy position) is replaced by **Accounting hierarchy** / **VDT hierarchy**, **Reporting Node** / **Activity Node** as non-leaf types, and **Posting GL Account** / **Posting Activity Account** as the two hierarchies' respective (non-identical) leaf types.

**Considered and rejected**: leaves being the exact same `GLFact`-backed rows with just a second parent pointer (the original framing of this ADR) — rejected once real seed data showed VDT leaves are independently formula-computed activity lines that reference a GL account for context, not the account itself, and don't need to sum back to it. **Considered and rejected**: enforcing strict allocation (Posting Activity Account amounts scaled/validated to sum exactly to their anchor GL total) — rejected in favor of independent estimates, since the discrepancy itself is the point of building a VDT view at all.

**Open items**: pilot scope is Cost of Revenue + Revenue only; every other branch shares its existing accounting node unchanged, reconciling exactly since nothing there has an independent VDT estimate. Extending activity-based structure to another branch later is pure data (new Activity Node / Posting Activity Account rows), no schema change beyond what this ADR introduces.

**Status**: accepted
