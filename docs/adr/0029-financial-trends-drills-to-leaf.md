# Financial Trends drills to the Posting GL Account leaf; Operational Drivers reparented under their leaf

ADR-0022 had Financial Performance ("Financial Trends") stop at the Reporting Node/Root level — Posting GL Account leaves (1083 of them) were deliberately never rendered as their own rows, matching the old mock's curated summary-only P&L. That floor is now removed: `buildDisplayRows` (`gl-client.ts`) walks all the way to the Posting GL Account leaf, using the same collapsible-row mechanism Reporting Nodes already had (a node is collapsible whenever some row names it as `group`), so leaves appear as one more expandable level rather than a new UI concept. No change was needed in `gl_tree.py` — a Posting GL Account's `actual` was already computed from its own `gl_fact` rows, never by summing children, so exposing its `childIds` doesn't change its value, only what's visible below it.

The 12 `Operational Driver` nodes (ADR-0023) were re-parented from their revenue Reporting Node (e.g. `PNL-0003`) to the single Posting GL Account leaf they most directly explain (e.g. `4010100100`, "Revenue-Time Charter") — see `OPERATIONAL_DRIVERS` in `seed.py`. This makes "drill to the leaf, then to the operational drivers beneath it" literal: leaf → driver is now a real parent/child edge, not a display-only grouping. Only those 6 leaves have driver children; the other ~1077 leaves are terminal rows with no further decomposition, same as before. VDT Explorer's per-node "no further decomposition modelled" dead-end (ADR-0013) now surfaces one level deeper (at the leaf) for these 6 accounts instead of at the Reporting Node — unchanged for everything else, and out of scope for this change.

Rejected: keeping drivers parented to the Reporting Node and only changing the Financial Trends UI to visually nest them under a leaf — this would leave "leaf" and "driver" living at the same tree depth server-side, incoherent with the GL/FSI hierarchy contract (ADR-0022) that treats `childIds` as the single source of nesting used by every screen (Financial Trends, VDT Explorer, Driver Diagnostic).

Drilling to the leaf also exposed a real data-shape problem: `PNL-0021` ("Manpower Cost") had 193 Posting GL Account leaves as direct children — one flat, unscannable list once expanded. Regrouped in `docs/anaplan_is_master_data.csv` under 11 synthetic Reporting Nodes (`PNL-0088`..`PNL-0098`: Crew Costs, Crew Recruitment & Training, Crew Relocation & Travel, Shore Staff Salary/Wages & Bonus, Shore Staff Allowances, Statutory Contributions, Staff Training & Development, Staff Medical & Welfare, Staff Recruitment/Subscriptions & Clubs, Gratuity & Separation, Contract & Subcontract Labour), classified by regexing each leaf's SAP description — the CSV's own naming convention (`COS-Crew costs-*`, `COS-Co Ctrbn to *`, etc.) already grouped cleanly by nature. Purely a hierarchy change: same 1083 leaves, same `gl_fact` rows, same rollup totals — only `Parent Code`/`Hierarchy Level` on those 193 rows and the 11 new node rows changed. Nothing else in the 1171-node CSV was regrouped; other Reporting Nodes with large flat leaf lists are unchanged unless a similar readability problem is raised for them.

On Financial Trends specifically, everything below hierarchy level 1 (i.e. every Reporting Node/Root below NPAT's direct children, and all Posting GL Account leaves and their drivers) now starts collapsed by default — a deliberate choice to keep the initial view minimal and force explicit drill-down, not just to hide the leaf level.

**Status**: accepted
