# Zeteo Diagnostic

Zeteo is a diagnostic model for explaining financial performance through Accounting, Value Drivers, and Drivers. It is one bounded context: its modules use one shared meaning for its business terms, while consuming authoritative Company and Accounting reference data from upstream systems.

## Language

**Zeteo Diagnostic**:
The bounded context that turns financial and operational data into financial-performance diagnostics. Accounting, VDT, Driver Model, and Diagnostics are internal modules of this one model, not separate bounded contexts.
_Avoid_: treating a module, database table, or API route as a bounded context by itself.

**Authoritative source**:
The upstream ERP or master-data platform that owns the canonical Company, Company Hierarchy, Accounting hierarchy, and Posting GL Account definitions. Zeteo consumes a read-only representation for diagnostic use; it does not become the system of record for those definitions.
_Avoid_: calling Zeteo's local imported copy the master record or treating it as the source of truth.

**Diagnostic read model**:
Zeteo's local, read-only representation of Company and Accounting reference data received from its Authoritative Source, shaped for diagnostic queries rather than source-system administration. It is not a live query into the ERP and does not write changes back to it; operational processes, not Zeteo, govern its import and retention.
_Avoid_: a local master-data system or a live ERP proxy.

**POC serving layer**:
The single SQLite application model that exposes Zeteo's approved computation and frontend contracts during the proof of concept. Its consumer-facing facts and reference data are provisional Gold candidates, not certified Gold: production certification requires governed source lineage, data-quality rules, ownership, and refresh controls.
_Avoid_: claiming that every table read by the frontend is Gold; treating the POC as a physical Bronze/Silver/Gold implementation.

**Zeteo-owned master data**:
The canonical definitions maintained within Zeteo: Period, VDT Hierarchy Node, VDT Account, and Driver. Period is a common reference model used by both Accounting and VDT; the other definitions belong to the diagnostic model rather than imported ERP reference data.
_Avoid_: assuming all master data comes from the ERP or that Zeteo may write back its definitions to it.

**Accounting hierarchy**:
The GL/FSI reporting tree — Reporting Root (`NPAT`), Reporting Nodes (subtotal categories grouped by GL nature, e.g. Manpower Cost, Materials And Supplies, Repairs And Maintenance), Posting GL Accounts as leaves. Trends is its home screen. Stored as two dimensions since ADR-0048 — `gl_hierarchy` (Root/Node structure) and `gl_account` (leaves) — mirroring Company hierarchy's split from Company below; `nodeType` (Reporting Root/Reporting Node/Posting GL Account) is derived at serve time from table membership and `parent_code IS NULL`, not stored. See `docs/adr/0033-vdt-activity-hierarchy-alongside-accounting.md` and `docs/adr/0048-gl-account-hierarchy-split.md`.
_Avoid_: "VDT" or "VDT node" for this tree — that term now belongs to the VDT hierarchy below; this one is Accounting; assuming `node_type` is a stored column — it's computed, not persisted, since ADR-0048.

**VDT hierarchy**:
The activity-based tree — same Reporting Root and, outside the pilot scope, the same shared Reporting Nodes as the Accounting hierarchy, but within Cost of Revenue and Revenue its mid-tier is VDT Hierarchy Nodes (e.g. Crew Pay and Benefits Cost, Crew Traveling and Welfare Cost) instead of Reporting Nodes, terminating in its own VDT Account leaves rather than reusing Posting GL Accounts. VDT Explorer is its home screen. See `docs/adr/0033-vdt-activity-hierarchy-alongside-accounting.md`.
_Avoid_: assuming VDT hierarchy totals reconcile to the Accounting hierarchy's totals within the pilot scope — they don't, by design (see VDT Account); only outside the pilot scope, where nodes are literally shared, do they.

**VDT Hierarchy Node**:
The VDT hierarchy's non-leaf node type within Cost of Revenue/Revenue, replacing Reporting Node there — a named activity (not a GL-nature category) that VDT Accounts roll up into. Coded `V` + 9 digits (branch digit + up to four 2-digit nesting-position segments, e.g. `V201000000`); the code is for human legibility only, `Parent Code` is the actual source of structure. Renamed from Activity Node — see `docs/adr/0047-vdt-hierarchy-account-rename.md`. See `docs/adr/0033-vdt-activity-hierarchy-alongside-accounting.md`.
_Avoid_: "VDT node" (retired, ambiguous between the two hierarchies — say VDT Hierarchy Node specifically); "Activity Node" (retired — see `docs/adr/0047-vdt-hierarchy-account-rename.md`).

**VDT Account**:
The VDT hierarchy's terminal line within Cost of Revenue/Revenue — a named item (e.g. "Senior officer nationality mix") with its own Driver Formula computing its own RM amount, the same shape as a Driver Formula target (see below) rather than a `Financial` row. Carries an `FA GL` pointer to the real Posting GL Account it's conceptually explaining — a display/reconciliation anchor, **not identity**: the pointer can be many-to-one (several VDT Accounts anchoring to one GL account, e.g. `backend/data/configuration/vdt/hierarchy.csv`'s Senior/Junior/Ratings officer nationality mix all anchoring to `5100100100`), and its amount is an independent estimate **not required to sum** to the anchor's real total — the gap between them is the diagnostic signal the Reconciliation screen surfaces, not an error to close. Coded separately from VDT Hierarchy Node, flat sequential `VA` + 8 digits (e.g. `VA00000001`), no positional encoding. Always anchors to a `gl_account` (leaf) code, never an interior `gl_hierarchy` node — see `docs/adr/0048-gl-account-hierarchy-split.md`. Renamed from Posting Activity Account — see `docs/adr/0047-vdt-hierarchy-account-rename.md`. See `docs/adr/0033-vdt-activity-hierarchy-alongside-accounting.md`.
_Avoid_: treating the `FA GL` pointer as a parent/allocation relationship (implies the amounts must reconcile — they don't); "VDT node" (retired); "Posting Activity Account" (retired — see `docs/adr/0047-vdt-hierarchy-account-rename.md`).

**Reporting Node**:
The Accounting hierarchy's non-leaf subtotal node type (e.g. Manpower Cost, Repairs And Maintenance) — grouped by GL nature. Shared verbatim by the VDT hierarchy outside Cost of Revenue/Revenue. A row in `gl_hierarchy` with a non-null `parent_code`; the Reporting Root (`NPAT`) is the one `gl_hierarchy` row where `parent_code` is null — see Accounting hierarchy and `docs/adr/0048-gl-account-hierarchy-split.md`.

**Posting GL Account**:
The Accounting hierarchy's leaf type — one real SAP GL code (e.g. `4010100100`), one `Financial` value. Within Cost of Revenue/Revenue, the VDT hierarchy does not reuse these as its own leaves (see VDT Account); outside that scope, both hierarchies share the same Reporting Node → Posting GL Account structure. May carry Driver/Driver Formula children (see below) — that decomposition is orthogonal to which hierarchy's tree the leaf sits in. A row in `gl_account`, whose `parent_code` always points at a `gl_hierarchy` node — see `docs/adr/0048-gl-account-hierarchy-split.md`.

**Driver**:
A reusable named quantity (e.g. Crew Complement, Payroll Rate) valued per company × month × source (actual/budget/prior-year), with no fixed tree position of its own — the same Driver can feed multiple Driver Formulas. A monetary Driver is denominated in that Company's local currency; a foreign-currency Driver cannot feed a local-currency target until currency conversion is modelled. A Driver is either terminal (a raw value) or itself computed by one or more Driver Formulas bound to it as their target, which is what lets driver decomposition recurse to any depth. A Driver with no Formula and not referenced as anyone's term (e.g. the legacy charter-rate/utilization drivers) carries an optional `displayed_under` pointer to the GL leaf it historically explained — a display-only anchor, not part of the compute graph. See `docs/adr/0030-driver-formula-computed-gl-values.md`.
_Avoid_: Operational Driver (retired `GLNode`-based concept this replaces).

**Driver Formula**:
A named expression, restricted to sum-of-products (ordered terms, each a chain of Drivers combined by `×` or `÷`, terms summed with an optional per-formula sign), that computes the value of exactly one target — a GL Posting Account leaf or another Driver. Multiple Formulas may drive the same target; the target's value is the sum of all of them, replacing any independently-fabricated fact row for that target. Formulas never contain raw numeric literals or nested/parenthesized sub-expressions — a constant is modelled as a flat-valued Driver. See `docs/adr/0030-driver-formula-computed-gl-values.md`.
_Avoid_: "formula" alone (say Driver Formula — this is a specific bound, sum-of-products expression, not a general spreadsheet formula); "calculation" (too generic).

**Target (of a Driver Formula)**:
The single GL Posting Account leaf or Driver whose value a Driver Formula computes. A target can have multiple Formulas bound to it (summed); a Driver can itself be a target for other Formulas, letting driver decomposition recurse.
_Avoid_: "output" (implies a UI artifact rather than the bound relationship it actually is).

**VDT Explorer**:
The screen family for browsing the **VDT hierarchy** (not Accounting), reached via the **Value Driver** nav item. Two independent view modes, each its own route — Ranked (`/vdt/:id`, a summary card + a children table ranked by contribution) and Tree (`/vdt-tree/:id`, a horizontal decomposition diagram). Ranked/Tree are not tabs of one screen; each has its own click model (Ranked rows open Driver Diagnostic directly; Tree nodes re-centre the diagram). Its landing page is VDT Trends (below), at bare `/vdt`; Ranked/Tree themselves stay nav-invisible, reached only by drilling in from Trends (or elsewhere). See `docs/adr/0033-vdt-activity-hierarchy-alongside-accounting.md`, `docs/adr/0038-vdt-comparison-route-rename.md`, and `docs/adr/0039-vdt-trends-landing-page.md`.
_Avoid_: VDT Explorer for the tree view alone — the term covers Ranked and Tree both. Also avoid conflating it with Financial below — they're separate nav branches over different hierarchies, not one screen family.

**VDT Trends**:
The screen at bare `/vdt`, reached via the Value Driver nav item's dropdown ("Trends") or by clicking "Value Driver" itself — VDT Explorer's real landing page, filling the gap `docs/adr/0038` left open. A statement table walking the full VDT hierarchy from the same anchor VDT Variance Analysis/Reconciliation use (`V201000000`, SOC Crew Cost), 12 (or fewer, see Trailing window below) monthly columns, with per-cell drill-down into Ranked (`/vdt/:id?period=...`). No charts — unlike Accounting's Trends (below), which pairs its statement with KPI cards, a line chart, and a profit-bridge waterfall, this screen is the table alone; a 12-column table already shows month-over-month movement without one. Context Bar here is Company, an Actual/Budget data-selection chip, a Period control with two modes (Financial Year / Trailing — see Trailing window), Cumulative toggle, and Monetary display scale — no comparison chip, no node picker. Budget is a real, live option; the table itself still renders the not-comparable dash (`—`) for it, but Trend Analysis (below) now computes a real monthly Budget series under the hood. See `docs/adr/0039-vdt-trends-landing-page.md`, `docs/adr/0040-vdt-trends-trend-analysis-narrative.md`, and `docs/adr/0042-vdt-trends-trailing-window.md`.
_Avoid_: confusing with Accounting's Trends (`/financial` — different hierarchy, and has charts this screen deliberately omits); assuming the table's Budget column shows real figures — it still renders dashes, even though Trend Analysis has real Budget data behind it now; assuming the screen always shows exactly 12 columns — Trailing mode can show fewer.

**Trailing window**:
VDT Trends' second Period mode (alongside Financial Year), added by `docs/adr/0042-vdt-trends-trailing-window.md` — a 12-month window ending at a user-picked anchor month, sliding freely across the FY24/FY25/FY26 sibling-root boundary (see Period) rather than being locked to one fiscal year. The anchor picker has no lower bound: picking an anchor close to the earliest seeded data produces a **partial window** of fewer than 12 months (down to 1), a deliberate design choice matching how production will bootstrap from zero operational history rather than a bug to guard against. Columns are labelled calendar-style (`Sep '25`) rather than the bare month name Financial Year mode uses, since a trailing window can repeat a month name across two fiscal years. Budget source is allowed even when the window stitches two different fiscal years' independently-prorated annual budget baselines — no caveat or visual flag is shown for the seam. Trend Analysis (below) works in this mode too, narrating from accurate window metadata with no special-cased caveat text.
_Avoid_: "Rolling" (reserved for forecasting, e.g. a rolling forecast — this feature only ever looks backward over closed/seeded actuals, never forward); assuming a minimum window size is enforced — there isn't one.

**Trend Analysis**:
The on-demand, LLM-generated narrative on VDT Trends, triggered by a button above the statement table — the VDT Trends analogue of Variance Analysis, but scanning the whole selected window (a fiscal year, or a Trailing window — see above) for what stands out rather than explaining a two-point diff. Detection is server-computed, not LLM-judged: a node is flagged when its month-over-month change exceeds 15% and its magnitude exceeds 5% of the root's total for that month; the LLM only narrates nodes the backend already flagged, and always explains a flagged movement via its Driver Formula terms (e.g. crew headcount, travel/crew-movement counts, rates) rather than the dollar figure alone — emphasizing the operational cause over the financial outcome. Always reads the underlying monthly (non-cumulative) series regardless of the Cumulative toggle, and works on whichever series the Actual/Budget chip selects (Budget's monthly series is computed the same way Actual's is, via `DriverEngine` with `source="budget"`). Output is one headline plus up to 6 bullets, variable rather than fixed-count. See `docs/adr/0040-vdt-trends-trend-analysis-narrative.md` and `docs/adr/0042-vdt-trends-trailing-window.md`.
_Avoid_: confusing with Variance Analysis (VDT Variance Analysis's two-period diff explanation) — Trend Analysis is a whole-window scan over one tree, not a delta between two chosen periods; implying the LLM decides what's significant — the 15%/5% flagging is deterministic backend logic, the LLM's job is only to explain flagged items.

**Reconciliation**:
The screen at `/vdt/reconciliation`, reached via the Value Driver nav item's dropdown, comparing VDT hierarchy amounts against their Accounting-hierarchy counterparts. A single table walks the VDT hierarchy from the same anchor VDT Variance Analysis's root uses (`V201000000`, SOC Crew Cost), three columns per row — VDT amount, Accounting GL amount, Delta (VDT − Accounting). Only VDT Account leaves carry a real Accounting GL amount, looked up via their `FA GL` pointer; VDT Hierarchy Node rows, including the root, render GL and Delta blank since no same-code Accounting node exists for them (see VDT Account). No color coding or favourable/adverse scoring on Delta, and no page-level total — an aggregate would hit the same double-counting ambiguity that already rules out a GL figure at the VDT Hierarchy Node level. Context Bar is limited to Company, Period, and a YTD toggle (periodic movement vs cumulative-to-date, same convention as elsewhere; on by default here), no node picker. Backed by `GET /api/vdt/reconciliation`. See `docs/adr/0037-reconciliation-single-vdt-hierarchy-table.md`.
_Avoid_: confusing with Comparison (`/financial/compare` — different hierarchy, delta between two *periods* not two hierarchies) or VDT Variance Analysis (also period-over-period, but VDT vs VDT across time rather than VDT vs Accounting at one point in time); implying Delta is colored or scored — it's a plain number, the gap itself is the diagnostic signal, not a favourable/adverse verdict.

**Financial (nav item)**:
The top-level nav item (`docs/adr/0031-financial-comparison-page.md`) whose hover dropdown holds two screens — **Trends** and **Comparison** — each documented below. Clicking "Financial" itself (rather than a dropdown item) is a direct link to Trends.
_Avoid_: calling this "VDT Explorer's landing state" — that was the wireframe's framing, superseded once Financial got its own nav item and route, separate from Value Driver.

**Trends**:
The full-P&L overview screen at `/financial`, reached via the Financial nav item's dropdown (or by clicking "Financial" itself). Shows the whole **Accounting hierarchy** statement (Freight & Charter Revenue down to NPAT, actual/budget/prior-year, subtotals shaded) plus 4 KPI cards and two illustrative charts (Revenue/Cost of Revenue/OPEX bar chart, profit-bridge waterfall). Every row down to the Posting GL Account leaf is expandable in place, and leaves with Driver/Driver Formula children expand one level further to show those; everything below NPAT's direct children starts collapsed, so the statement opens minimal and the user drills down explicitly — see `docs/adr/0029-financial-trends-drills-to-leaf.md`. It is not a VDT Explorer view mode and does not share tree data with it once Cost of Revenue/Revenue diverge (see `docs/adr/0033-vdt-activity-hierarchy-alongside-accounting.md`) — it's a separate landing over the Accounting hierarchy specifically. Clicking a leaf row still routes into VDT Explorer (`/vdt/:id`) for that same GL code, since leaves are shared between both hierarchies. See `docs/adr/0013-financial-performance-landing.md`.
_Avoid_: "Financial Performance" (retired term — this screen is just "Trends" now, "Financial" is the nav item one level up); implying it shares tree data with VDT Explorer — since ADR-0033 they're two different hierarchies over the same leaves, not one shared tree.

**Comparison**:
The screen at `/financial/compare`, reached via the Financial nav item's dropdown, for comparing a single **comparison node**'s value between two same-grain periods (both Month, both Quarter, or both Year — never mixed). Renders a delta profit bridge (one waterfall from Period A's total to Period B's total, one bar per direct GL child showing its change, colored favourable/adverse by polarity) and a 3-column table (Period A / Period B / Delta) for the comparison node's full subtree. Backed by `GET /api/financial/comparison`, which diffs server-side and returns only the comparison node's subtree. See `docs/adr/0031-financial-comparison-page.md`.
_Avoid_: "diff view", "delta page" — the screen is "Comparison".

**Comparison node**:
The Accounting hierarchy node a Comparison screen is anchored to — the root of the subtree its bridge and table both render. Restricted to Reporting Root/Reporting Node types; a Posting GL Account can never be a comparison node, even one with Driver/Driver Formula children, since those children's units (days, usd-per-day, ratio, etc.) aren't RM-comparable and can't feed a bridge. See `docs/adr/0031-financial-comparison-page.md`.
_Avoid_: "anchor node", "root node" (ambiguous with a node's own `Reporting Root` type).

**VDT Variance Analysis**:
The screen at `/vdt/variance`, reached via the Value Driver nav item's dropdown ("Variance") — a Cost Bridge and statement table made comparison-aware via the Context Bar's `vs Last Year` / `vs This Year` chip options, the VDT-hierarchy analogue of Comparison above, but scoped to SOC Crew Cost rather than an arbitrary comparison node. It compares two same-grain Periods at Year, Quarter, or Month: `vs This Year` shows two pickers restricted to the current fiscal year, while `vs Last Year` shows one picker and auto-derives its matching prior-year Period. Year has no `vs This Year` option because one fiscal year has only one Year Period; it always compares to the matching prior year. A `Compare by` control lets users choose Month, Quarter, or Year without exposing the technical term "grain"; changing it reseeds the newest valid same-type draft comparison. The draft comparison controls, including mode and YTD, update the Cost Bridge and statement only when Context Bar Apply commits them. It initially remains a YTD Month `vs Last Year` comparison of the latest available period. Its YTD toggle leaves a Year unchanged, compares a Quarter alone when off, and compares fiscal-year-to-quarter-end totals when on. Backed by `GET /api/vdt/comparison`. Formerly called "VDT Statement comparison", then "VDT Comparison" (served at `/vdt/compare`) — renamed again because "Comparison" named the two-period mechanism rather than what the page is for (surfacing and explaining variance), the same reasoning that named its narrative panel Variance Analysis (below). See `docs/adr/0034-vdt-statement-comparison-and-movement-narration.md`, `docs/adr/0038-vdt-comparison-route-rename.md`, and `docs/adr/0042-vdt-variance-same-grain-period-comparison.md`.
_Avoid_: "VDT Statement", "VDT Statement comparison", or "VDT Comparison" (retired names for the same screen); confusing with Comparison (the separate `/financial/compare` screen, different hierarchy) — Financial's dropdown is Trends/Comparison, Value Driver's is now Trends/Variance/Reconciliation/Sensitivity, deliberately no longer sharing the "Comparison" label.

**Variance Analysis**:
The on-demand, LLM-generated explanation shown beside VDT Variance Analysis's Cost Bridge once a comparison is active — a headline sentence plus zero to four bullets naming contributors and, where the leaf is Driver Formula-driven, the driver-level cause (e.g. quantity vs. rate) rather than just the dollar delta. Generated from a backend-computed prompt payload (the selected material VDT Accounts plus Driver Formula terms, all numbers pre-computed server-side) so the LLM narrates given facts rather than computing its own arithmetic. Its response keeps monetary references structured and unscaled so the client can apply the screen's current Company local currency and Monetary display scale without another LLM call. First LLM integration in this codebase — backend-mediated (`POST /api/vdt/variance-analysis`, `gpt-4o-mini`), never auto-fired on period changes, cached in-memory per period pair, and fails in isolation from the bridge/table if unavailable. Formerly called "Movement Narration". See `docs/adr/0034-vdt-statement-comparison-and-movement-narration.md`, `docs/adr/0038-vdt-comparison-route-rename.md`, and `docs/adr/0041-vdt-variance-analysis-financial-selection-operational-explanation.md`.
Financial impact deterministically selects material **VDT Account** contributors: rank leaves by absolute two-period delta and retain at most four whose delta is at least 10% of gross subtree movement (the sum of absolute leaf deltas). The analysis returns exactly one bullet for every selected contributor. A Driver Formula term is changed when its unit-appropriate displayed Period A and B values differ, with no cross-unit percentage threshold; each bullet must cite every changed term and may cite an unchanged quantity or rate when that contrast isolates the cause. “Because” and “driven by” are allowed only for Driver Formula terms that compute the selected contributor. Where no such term is modelled, it says that operational decomposition is unavailable; where a formula exists but no term changed at displayed precision, it says that the operational evidence is inconclusive. Neither case permits an invented cause. The LLM explains each selected contributor through these terms, not by restating its financial variance.
Driver evidence is rendered in its native operational unit and is unaffected by the monetary display scale. Currency-rate terms carry the selected Company's ISO currency and cadence (e.g. `MYR/month`), with thousands separators.
If no contributor meets the threshold, the analysis says that movement was distributed across smaller items and returns no bullets; it never lowers the threshold or invents an individual operational cause.
When selected contributors move in opposing directions, the headline explicitly identifies that they offset, even if the root's net variance is small.
_Avoid_: "Movement Narration" (retired name for the same panel); "Operational Driver" (say Driver Formula term); implying it reconciles or validates the numbers — it explains figures the backend already computed, it doesn't compute or check them itself; confusing with Trend Analysis (VDT Trends' analogue, a whole-year scan rather than a two-period diff — see above).

**VDT Sensitivity Analysis**:
The screen at `/vdt/sensitivity`, reached via the Value Driver nav item's dropdown ("Sensitivity") — ranks a company's terminal Drivers by their elasticity (see below) with respect to NPAT, over a Financial Year or Trailing window (the same period picker VDT Trends uses). The user picks a **scope** node (any VDT node, defaulting to the whole book at NPAT) that determines which terminal Drivers get tested; every tested Driver's impact is always measured through to NPAT itself, never to a nearer parent. On-demand only (a button triggers the run, no auto-fire, no caching — the user-configurable bump % would fragment a cache key too finely to be useful), rendered as a top-10 tornado chart (bars coloured favourable/adverse by polarity, the same convention Comparison's profit bridge uses) plus a data table. No LLM narrative — chart and table only. Progress is streamed via Server-Sent Events, since the total number of engine reruns (terminal Drivers in scope × 2 bump directions × months in the window) is cheap to compute upfront and gives a meaningful "N of M" progress bar; this is the first streaming-response endpoint in the app. See `docs/adr/0043-vdt-sensitivity-analysis-elasticity-ranking.md`.
_Avoid_: implying this finds an "optimal" value for a Driver (e.g. an optimal price) — that requires modelling a behavioural relationship between two Drivers, which this screen does not do; it ranks structural impact on the formula tree as it exists today, nothing more. That behavioural, optimum-finding capability is the future Simulation feature, not this screen.

**Elasticity (Driver Sensitivity)**:
The impact metric VDT Sensitivity Analysis ranks by: for a given terminal Driver, the % change in NPAT resulting from a % change in that Driver's value, computed by perturbation — overriding the Driver's value, re-running `DriverEngine` through to NPAT, and diffing against baseline — rather than by closed-form calculus. Chosen over a raw-dollar impact metric because Drivers carry incompatible units (headcount, currency-per-month, ratio, etc.) that a %→% metric makes comparable on one scale. See `docs/adr/0043-vdt-sensitivity-analysis-elasticity-ranking.md`.
_Avoid_: confusing with a general "sensitivity analysis" that tests behavioural relationships between Drivers (e.g. price affecting volume) — this is structural, one Driver at a time, holding all others fixed.

**Driver Diagnostic (workspace)**:
The tabbed screen (`/diagnostic/:id/:tab`) for one node — Diagnose / Benchmark / Root Cause & Mitigation tabs, sharing one breadcrumb and context. Hierarchy-agnostic: the `:id` is a GL code, Reporting Node, or VDT Hierarchy Node, whichever hierarchy the user drilled in from. Opened either from an exception, a VDT Explorer row, or a Home driver link.
_Avoid_: driver detail page, node detail.

**Context bar**:
The persistent strip pinned across every screen showing Business / Period / vs Budget filter chips, an Apply button, plus, where relevant, the breadcrumb back to the tree root. The three-way comparison chip (`vs Budget` / `vs Last Year` / `vs This Year`, `vs Last Year` renamed from `vs Prior Year`) remains fully decorative everywhere per `docs/adr/0005-inert-filters-live-navigation.md`, **except on VDT Variance Analysis**, where `vs Last Year` and `vs This Year` are live (see VDT Variance Analysis above; `vs Budget` stays inert there too) — see `docs/adr/0034-vdt-statement-comparison-and-movement-narration.md`. **VDT Trends** carries a separate, unrelated Actual/Budget data-selection chip (not the three-way comparison chip, which it omits entirely) — see VDT Trends above and `docs/adr/0039-vdt-trends-landing-page.md`. The Business picker shows the full Company hierarchy (variable-depth, MISC Group root, down through Company leaves — see Company hierarchy) but permits only Company selection (see Company local currency); it and the Period picker (see Period below) stage a draft, and Apply commits both and refetches (see `docs/adr/0027-context-bar-apply-button.md`). On live Company-scoped monetary screens, the Monetary display scale selector also lives here but applies immediately without Apply. The breadcrumb is live navigation, unaffected by Apply.

**Fiscal year**:
The integer accounting year (for example, `2026`) — the `Year` table's key, and the `year` value stamped on every `Financial`/`DriverFact` row. MISC fiscal years are calendar-aligned today, but that alignment is configuration, not a redefinition of the term. See `docs/adr/0051-period-year-and-period-integers.md`.
_Avoid_: calendar year when identifying a postable accounting period; treating `FYxx` as a stored identity — that was the retired `period.code` format (see Period), display formatting only now.

**Period**:
An integer 1–12 identifying a fiscal-relative month within a Fiscal year (period 1 is that year's first month; today that's still calendar January, but the two are conceptually independent — see Fiscal year). A `year` + `period` pair is what's actually stored on a fact row and what scopes actual/budget/prior-year figures on VDT Explorer and Driver Diagnostic via the Context Bar's Period chip, an accordion-style Year/Quarter/Month dropdown (deliberately not searchable like the Business chip — see `docs/adr/0026`). Quarter is never stored — it's looked up from the static `Period` reference table (`(period-1)/3 + 1`), and the picker's Year→Quarter→Month tree is computed server-side each request by joining `Year` × `Period`, not read off a stored hierarchy. Three fiscal years — `2024`, `2025`, `2026` — exist as independent `Year` rows; Quarter/Month labels are year-qualified in the UI ("Jan FY24") since the bare month/quarter name repeats across years, formatted `FYxx`-style for display even though the stored value is a plain int (see Fiscal year). "Prior year" always means the real `year - 1`'s own actuals, not a separately-stored source — see `docs/adr/0032-single-company-multi-year-focus.md`. Trends is the one screen the chip doesn't scope — the chip is still there and interactive, but that page's Income Statement always shows all 12 months of whichever year is current and its KPI cards are always labelled "YTD", regardless of the current pick. Comparison doesn't use the Period chip at all — it has its own two same-grain period pickers (Period A / Period B), independent of the Context Bar, which can span any two years. Only a Month-grain `(year, period)` pair is **postable** — the only grain a GL fact can be recorded against; Year and Quarter exist purely as reporting rollups derived at query time, never as their own postable rows, the same relationship Reporting Nodes have to Posting GL Accounts in the Accounting hierarchy (and VDT Hierarchy Nodes have to Posting GL Accounts in the VDT hierarchy). VDT Trends uses its own two-mode variant of this picker (see Trailing window) — Financial Year mode lists only the three fiscal years with no Quarter/Month drill-down (unchanged since ADR-0039), while Trailing mode picks a single anchor Month and derives a 12-month (or fewer) window backward from it via `(year, period)` arithmetic, which can cross a fiscal-year boundary that this picker otherwise keeps separate everywhere else. See `docs/adr/0025-period-hierarchical-master-data.md` (Month-only-postable origin), `docs/adr/0026-period-chip-live-universal-picker.md`, `docs/adr/0032-single-company-multi-year-focus.md`, `docs/adr/0039-vdt-trends-landing-page.md`, `docs/adr/0042-vdt-trends-trailing-window.md`, and `docs/adr/0051-period-year-and-period-integers.md` (`year`/`period` integer identity, replacing the `FYxx-Mxx` hierarchy).
_Avoid_: "period" for an arbitrary date range — it always means one specific `(year, period)` pair; "postable" for Year/Quarter (only a Month-grain pair is postable); "the fiscal year" singular — there are three now; "period code" or `FYxx-Mxx` as if a string identity were still stored (see `docs/adr/0051`).

**Current Actual Period**:
The Month-only `(year, period)` pair in App Settings (`current_actual_year` + `current_actual_period`) that marks the global boundary between historical Actuals and the Budget baseline for Simulation. It is independent of a user's frontend Period selection: Simulation preserves Actuals up to and including this Month, then starts from the following Month using Financial Budget and Driver Budget values.
_Avoid_: active period (ambiguous with a user's selected Period); Last Actual Period; Actuals Through; a Company-specific, Year, or Quarter boundary.

**App Settings**:
The one global `app_settings` row holding runtime business settings shared across Zeteo capabilities, beginning with Current Actual Period. It is for application-wide business behavior, not per-user preferences, source facts, or deployment secrets.
_Avoid_: Simulation settings; a JSON configuration file; per-user settings.

**POC Budget Import**:
A CSV dataset loaded by a script after selecting one calendar Year and one dataset type: Financial Budget (Posting GL Account amounts) or Driver Budget (raw Driver values). Financial CSVs use `company_code,year,period,gl_account_code,amount`; Driver CSVs use `company_code,year,period,driver_code,value`, where `year` is a four-digit calendar year and `period` is 1–12 — the same `year`/`period` identity Zeteo stores internally (see Period), so no code-mapping step is needed. The script uses only rows matching the selected Year; source is always Budget and a Driver's unit comes from Zeteo master data. The dummy-data POC validates CSV structure, rejects duplicate Company × code × month rows, and verifies that supplied master-data codes exist locally, but does not require complete annual coverage. After explicit confirmation, it atomically replaces every budget fact in its selected Fiscal Year and dataset type with the matching file rows.
_Avoid_: budget release; admin upload page; annual total (the import consists of monthly postable facts).

**POC Actual Import**:
A CSV dataset loaded by the admin script for one selected Company, Current Actual Period, and dataset type: Financial Actual (Posting GL Account amounts) or Driver Actual (raw Driver values). Financial CSVs use `company_code,year,period,gl_account_code,amount`; Driver CSVs use `company_code,year,period,driver_code,value`. Only rows whose Company and Month (`year`, `period`) match the selections are imported; other rows are skipped and reported, while zero matching rows reject the file. After validation and explicit confirmation, it atomically replaces Actual facts in precisely that Company × Month × dataset-type scope.
_Avoid_: importing arbitrary past Actuals; a whole-Fiscal-Year Actual replacement.

**Company hierarchy**:
The Business picker's grouping structure above Company, stored as its own dimension (`company_hierarchy`, `hierarchy_kind = BU`), decoupled from the `company` table itself — see `docs/adr/0045-company-hierarchy-bu-legal-dimension.md`. Depth is variable, not a fixed number of tiers: `MISC Group` is always the root, but branches reach it at different depths — `MISCM` and `MMS` sit under an intermediate `MISC Marine` node before `MISC Group`, while `AET`, `ALAM`, `GAS`, `MHB`, `MISC`, and `OBU` parent directly to `MISC Group`. `hierarchy_kind` exists to let a second, unrelated Legal entity hierarchy (`hierarchy_kind = LEGAL`) share the same table shape later without touching BU's rows. Every node in this hierarchy is an expandable grouping for reaching Companies, never a selectable monetary scope (see Company local currency).
_Avoid_: assuming every Business Unit is the same number of hops from MISC Group — depth is per-branch, not fixed; treating `company_hierarchy` as part of the `company` table — Company is a leaf-only dimension that points *into* this hierarchy via `bu_node_code`, not a row within it (unlike the retired `company_node.node_type` scheme in ADR-0028).

**Business Unit (BU)**:
A grouping node within the Company hierarchy (`hierarchy_kind = BU`) — AET, ALAM, Gas Business Unit (GAS), Malaysia Marine and Heavy Engineering (MHB), MISC corporate (CORP), MISC Maritime Services (MMS), MISC Ship Management (MISCM), and Offshore Business Unit (OBU). Each BU groups one or more **Company** records (a specific legal entity, e.g. "AET Tanker Holdings Sdn. Bhd.") via that Company's required `bu_node_code` and is an expandable grouping rather than a selectable monetary scope. This is distinct from the 7 "solution divisions" MISC's public website markets (Petroleum and Product Shipping, Gas Assets and Solutions, etc.) — that's a marketing/segment framing, not the BU grouping the company data actually uses. Zeteo models BU, not the marketing divisions.
_Avoid_: "business segment" for BU (that's the website's marketing term, not this data's grouping); "subsidiary" alone for Company (BU is also a kind of grouping, be specific about which level); assuming a BU's own parent is always MISC Group directly — BU is always Company's immediate parent, but the BU's parent varies (MISC Marine for MISCM/MMS, MISC Group directly for the rest).

**Company local currency**:
The required ISO 4217 currency assigned to a specific Company and the denomination of that Company's monetary facts. Monetary presentation identifies it by its ISO code (e.g. MYR or USD), never an ambiguous currency symbol. A Company without one valid currency is invalid master data: ingestion fails and the application never substitutes a default. Monetary views are available at Company scope only; Business Unit and MISC Group remain visible as expandable groupings but cannot be selected as monetary scopes until currency conversion is modelled. A direct request using either grouping is rejected as an invalid monetary scope rather than interpreted as zero, Not-yet-modelled, or a Partial BU total.
_Avoid_: treating a Business Unit's or MISC Group's currency as the local currency of one contributing Company; adding unconverted amounts denominated in different currencies; Partial BU total (retired because BU/Group monetary scopes are unavailable).

**Absolute monetary amount**:
A monetary fact or monetary Driver rate stored as an exact decimal with two fractional digits in the smallest unscaled expression of its declared currency unit: one million is stored as 1,000,000.00 and a monthly salary rate of eighteen thousand as 18,000.00. Its Company local currency identifies the denomination; neither currency nor a presentation scale is encoded into the monetary unit itself. Driver Formulas evaluate with full decimal precision, then round a final monetary target once per Company × Month × Source to two decimals using round-half-up; aggregates sum those rounded target amounts. Non-monetary measures such as counts, percentages, days, and ratios remain stored in their own native units and retain the precision appropriate to that unit; monetary scaling is presentation only.
_Avoid_: million-scaled or thousand-scaled stored values; multiplying non-monetary measures merely because monetary storage changed.

**Monetary display scale**:
The single magnitude used to present every monetary amount on one Company-scoped screen, shared by all of that screen's monetary tables, bridges, trees, charts, cards, and narrative figures. Unless the user overrides it, the largest absolute monetary value already loaded for the screen—including collapsed and off-screen rows—selects units below 1,000, thousands from 1,000, millions from 1,000,000, or billions from 1,000,000,000; negative values are classified by magnitude, and an all-zero screen resolves to Units. Expanding or scrolling existing content therefore never changes Auto, while a fetch or navigation begins a new calculation. The Context bar owns the selector and exposes Auto's resolved band (e.g. `Scale: Auto (Thousands)`); its available choices are Auto, Units, Thousands, Millions, and Billions, and an explicit choice is authoritative even when it produces unusually large or small displayed numbers. Changing it reformats the current screen immediately and never participates in the Context bar's staged Apply workflow. An override belongs only to the current screen and resets to Auto when the user navigates to another screen or applies a different Company or Period. Every displayed monetary value uses exactly one decimal place. A visual's shared caption spells out currency and scale (e.g. `MYR thousands`), dense cells and axes show only the scaled number (e.g. `144.0`), and standalone cards, tooltips, or narrative amounts repeat them compactly (e.g. `MYR 144.0k`). Non-monetary Drivers retain their own units. These rules apply everywhere live Company-scoped monetary data appears, not only within VDT Explorer; Home's independent static prototype content is temporarily outside this rule.
_Avoid_: independently scaling individual values or visual components, which makes like-for-like amounts harder to compare.

**Not-yet-modelled (state)**:
The empty-state shown when a node (in either hierarchy) lacks full diagnostic data (trend/contribution/benchmark/root-cause), or when a node has no fact data for the selected Company (see Sampled company). Distinguishes "this doesn't exist yet" from a routing error. Always carries a link to the one fully-modelled example node, `PNL-0024` (Repairs And Maintenance, Cost of Revenue). See `docs/adr/0004-mock-data-depth.md` and `docs/adr/0024-gl-fact-data-company-sampling.md`.
_Avoid_: empty state (too generic — this is a specific, deliberate placeholder for unmodelled tree depth or unsampled scope, not an empty list/search result).

**Sampled company**:
The single company — `0190`, MISC Ship Management SB — that carries fabricated GL fact data, across three fiscal years (see Period). Selecting any other Company renders Not-yet-modelled rather than fabricated figures; Business Unit and MISC Group are not selectable monetary scopes (see Company local currency). Previously 9 companies (3 per Business Unit); narrowed to one for a coherent, designed dataset rather than noise spread thin — see `docs/adr/0024-gl-fact-data-company-sampling.md` and `docs/adr/0032-single-company-multi-year-focus.md`.
_Avoid_: modelled company (overloads "modelled," already used for diagnostic depth in Not-yet-modelled).

**Attention item (exception)**:
The single highlighted card on Home surfacing the largest adverse variance needing investigation. Distinct from a "top adverse driver" (a plain ranked list row) — an attention item is the one thing Home foregrounds for immediate action.

**Leading indicator / lagging indicator**:
Leading indicators (e.g. maintenance backlog, off-hire days) signal what's coming; lagging indicators (the KPI row, variance tables) confirm what already happened. Rendered with a distinct visual treatment (dashed blue) and never mixed into the same list as lagging KPIs.

**FACT / AI HYPOTHESIS (root-cause entry)**:
The two states of a root-cause entry. FACT entries carry cited evidence and are shown with solid black styling. AI HYPOTHESIS entries carry an AI confidence level and rationale, are shown with dashed-blue styling, and remain AI-proposed until a human sets their status to Validated or Rejected — only a human can make that transition.
_Avoid_: finding, cause (ambiguous about provenance — always say which type).
